Buttons & calls to action

Five button styles. Each one has a job — using the right one is how visitors know what you want them to do.

The rule that matters

Green always means "Book a Chat". It's the one journey you want people on, so it keeps its own colour everywhere — header, menu, every page. Burgundy is the general-purpose action colour. Never use green for anything else, or it stops meaning anything.

1. Book a Chat — the primary journey

Book a Chat 
<a class="btn btn--chat"
   href="https://calendly.com/schuggies-ceilidhs/ceilidh-chat-how-a-ceilidh-will-work-for-your-wedding"
   target="_blank" rel="noopener">Book a Chat</a>

2. Primary — major action, when Book a Chat isn't the focus

Send a Message 
<a class="btn btn--primary" href="contact.html">Send a Message</a>

3. Sage — supporting action

Check Availability 
<a class="btn btn--sage"
   href="https://calendly.com/schuggies-ceilidhs/private-ceilidh-check-if-im-available-for-your-big-day"
   target="_blank" rel="noopener">Check Availability</a>

4. Ghost — quiet secondary action

See more reviews 
<a class="btn btn--ghost" href="testimonials.html">See more reviews</a>

5. On a dark background

Over a photo or a dark band, use btn--light and btn--ghost btn--on-dark — the normal versions disappear.

Prices & Packages FAQs 
<a class="btn btn--light" href="prices.html">Prices &amp; Packages</a>
<a class="btn btn--ghost btn--on-dark" href="faqs.html">FAQs</a>

Button rows

Buttons always sit inside a btn-row. It handles the spacing, and on a phone it arranges them two-by-two automatically.

Book a Chat Check Availability 
<div class="btn-row">
  <a class="btn btn--chat" href="…">Book a Chat</a>
  <a class="btn btn--sage" href="…">Check Availability</a>
</div>

To centre them, add btn-row--center:

Book a Chat Contact Me 
<div class="btn-row btn-row--center"> … </div>

The closing CTA strip

Every page ends with one of these. A page that just stops is a wasted visit.

Let's make your wedding unforgettable

Check availability for your date and lock in a price that won't budge until 2029.

Book a Chat Contact Me 
<section class="section section--closing"><div class="container">
  <div class="cta-strip reveal">
    <h2>Let's make your wedding unforgettable</h2>
    <p>Check availability for your date and lock in a price that won't
       budge until 2029.</p>
    <div class="btn-row btn-row--center">
      <a class="btn btn--chat" href="…">Book a Chat</a>
      <a class="btn btn--light" href="contact.html">Contact Me</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</div></section>

Things to avoid

  • Three different button colours side by side. Two at most, so the primary one wins.
  • More than four buttons in a hero. Four is already the ceiling.
  • Green for anything but Book a Chat.
  • Making a button smaller. Every button is at least 44px tall on a phone — that's fingertip size. Shrinking one makes it hard to tap.
