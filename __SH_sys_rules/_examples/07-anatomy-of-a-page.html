Anatomy of a page

Every page on this site is the same six pieces in the same order. Learn this once and you can build any page.

The skeleton, annotated

Each dashed box is one layer of the page. Nested boxes sit inside their parent.

<head>

The plumbing. Title, description, canonical, social tags, fonts, stylesheet. Five lines you edit, twenty you leave alone. Nothing here is visible on the page — it's what Google and WhatsApp read.

<div id="site-header-mount">

Empty on purpose. The top bar, logo and menu are injected here by main.js when the page loads. Delete this line and the page has no menu.

SECTION 1 — the hero

Who it's for, one promise, one obvious button. Three seconds to earn the scroll.

.hero__media

The photo layer. A <picture> block with five filenames — square for phones, wide for desktop, JPEG fallback.

.container.hero__inner

The text layer, sitting over the photo.

.eyebrow → h1 → .lead → .btn-row

Always this order. Small label, big promise, supporting sentence, buttons.

SECTION 2 — the pitch

Text beside a photo. What they actually get.

.container.split

Two columns on desktop, stacked on a phone — automatically. You do nothing.

SECTION 3 — the detail

A grid of features, or a fact list. The practical answers that stop someone leaving to check elsewhere.

SECTION 4 — proof

A review or two. Optional, but it's the cheapest persuasion on the page.

SECTION 5 — the closing CTA

Never optional. A page that just stops is a wasted visit. Always section--closing with a cta-strip.

<div id="site-footer-mount">

Empty on purpose. Footer, all its links, the WhatsApp button and the scroll-to-top are injected here.

<script src="…/main.js?b=NN">

The only script on the page. It builds the header and footer, wires the menu and accordions, and injects the chatbot itself. Never add a second one.

The same thing, as code

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en-GB">
<head>
  … title, description, canonical, og tags, fonts, stylesheet …
</head>
<body>

<div id="site-header-mount"></div>          <!-- menu appears here -->

<section class="hero grain hero--short">      <!-- 1. hero -->
  <div class="hero__media"><picture> … </picture></div>
  <div class="container hero__inner">
    <p class="eyebrow eyebrow--sage">Who it's for</p>
    <h1>The promise</h1>
    <p class="lead">The backup sentence.</p>
    <div class="btn-row"> … buttons … </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">                     <!-- 2. the pitch -->
  <div class="container split"> … text … photo … </div>
</section>

<section class="section section--paper2">     <!-- 3. the detail -->
  <div class="container"> … grid or fact-list … </div>
</section>

<section class="section section--sage">       <!-- 4. proof (optional) -->
  <div class="container"> … quote-feature … </div>
</section>

<section class="section section--closing">    <!-- 5. closing CTA -->
  <div class="container"><div class="cta-strip reveal"> … </div></div>
</section>

<div id="site-footer-mount"></div>          <!-- footer appears here -->
<script src="../assets/js/main.js?b=NN"></script>
</body>
</html>
That's the whole thing. Every page on the site — home, prices, weddings, all 56 blog posts — is this shape with different words in it. When something looks wrong, compare against this and find the piece that's missing.

The three things that break most often

1. A missing mount div

No menu, or no footer. Check both are present and spelled exactly:

<div id="site-header-mount"></div>
<div id="site-footer-mount"></div>

2. The wrong number of ../

Page loads as plain text with no styling.

Page locationPath to assets
index.htmlassets/…
pages/anything.html../assets/…
pages/blog/anything.html../../assets/…

3. An unclosed tag

Everything after it goes haywire. Every <div> needs its </div>. Paste the file into validator.w3.org/nu/ and it points straight at the line.
