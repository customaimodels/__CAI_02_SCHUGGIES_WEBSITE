Cards, features & steps

Four ways to present a set of short points. Pick by what the content actually is — they aren't interchangeable.

Which one?
Feature tiles — short selling points, no order.
Cards — things you can click through to.
Steps — a process, in order.
Fact list — practical details someone needs before booking.

1. Feature tiles

Six short selling points. The tick mark appears automatically.

Ceilidh first dance

Turn first-dance nerves into a joyful, guest-filled moment.

DJ or live band

Solo ceilidh DJ or a full live band — sizes to suit your space.

Every dance called

No experience needed. I walk everyone through each dance.

</div>

Keep it to one sentence each. Feature tiles are for scanning, not reading. And leave feature__icon empty — the tick is drawn by the stylesheet.

3. Numbered steps

For a process. The numbers are drawn for you — don't type them into the text.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Tell me your date and venue — or just your rough plans.

  2. 2

    Have a chat

    Twenty minutes, both of you ideally. It isn't a sales call.

  3. 3

    Lock it in

    Your price is fixed from that moment. No surprises.

4. Fact list

Practical details. Label on one side, answer on the other.

Travel East Midlands (NG, LE, DE) included. Beyond that, £0.55/mile plus £20/hour travel time.
Space needed Any size works — village hall, marquee, garden or castle hall.
Sound limits No problem. Ceilidhs peak around 85 dB, and bands can play fully acoustic.
