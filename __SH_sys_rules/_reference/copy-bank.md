# Copy Bank — approved words to steal

Pre-written, on-voice copy. **Take from here rather than writing from scratch** —
it's how the site keeps one voice instead of six.

## The voice, in one line

**Warm, direct, and a bit Scottish. Like Schuggie talking at a wedding fayre —
not a brochure, not a salesman.**

| Sounds like us | Doesn't |
|---|---|
| "No empty dancefloor. No awkward standing about." | "We pride ourselves on exceptional entertainment." |
| "Every dance is called, so nobody needs to know the steps." | "Our bespoke offering elevates your special day." |
| "It isn't a sales call." | "Book a complimentary consultation." |
| "Your gran, your mates and the shyest guest will all end up dancing." | "Suitable for guests of all ages." |

**Banned words:** bespoke · elevate · unforgettable experience · we pride
ourselves · seamless · curated · premium offering · reach out · touch base.

**Test:** would you say it out loud to someone at a fayre? If not, rewrite it.

## The objections, and the answers

Every one of these is a real reason someone hesitates. Use the answer, near
enough word for word.

| They're thinking | Say |
|---|---|
| "Our guests won't know how" | "Every dance is called — I guide everyone through the moves as we go. Beginners and seasoned dancers join in together, and making mistakes is half the fun." |
| "Our venue's too small / odd-shaped" | "Any size works — a cosy village hall, a marquee, a garden, an odd-shaped hotel room or a grand castle hall. Don't let venue size put you off." |
| "The venue has a sound limiter" | "No problem — I work with sound limiters all the time. My ceilidhs peak around 85 dB, well within most limits, and the bands can play fully acoustic outdoors." |
| "It'll go up in price before our date" | "Book for 2027, 2028 or 2029 and the price you see is the price you pay — full stop." |
| "Is it too far for you?" | "I cover the whole UK, including the Channel Islands — and I often work with local musicians to keep costs down." |
| "We're not ready to book yet" | "Get in touch as soon as you can, even just to run through ideas. You don't need a date or venue decided. It isn't a sales call." |
| "Will older guests join in?" | "Your gran, your mates and the shyest guest in the room will all end up dancing." |

## Emoji rules

Allowed in: blog posts (sparingly), the top bar.
**Never in:** headlines, buttons, page titles, meta descriptions.

A ceilidh is warm. It isn't a text message.

---

## The final test

Read it out loud. If you'd be embarrassed to say it to a couple across a table
at a wedding fayre, it doesn't go on the site.
